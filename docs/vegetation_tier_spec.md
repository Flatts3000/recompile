# Vegetation tier spec - Fertilizer scatters dump plants (reclamation rung 2)

Engineering spec for rung 2 (Vegetation) of the reclamation ladder. Design is locked in
`../trashlands/docs/design_decisions.md` (P2.4-R, P1.7-R); this is the build.

## What it is

Fertilizer (the Compost Heap's output) is a custom "fancy bonemeal": right-click reclaimed grass and it
scatters dump-friendly pioneer plants over a bonemeal-sized footprint, revealed over 3 seconds. The
plants **are** the vegetation - there is no separate "vegetated" blockstate. Live plants:

- are `frontier_cover` (encroachment strips a plant instead of reverting the grass under it - the
  defensive half of P1.7-R), and
- will be the terrain the Nursery rung (trees) requires later (rung gate, P2.4-R item 4).

Grass (rung 1, the Grass Spreader) is unchanged and is the prerequisite: Fertilizer no-ops on coarse dirt.

## Locked decisions (Jason, 2026-07-26)

1. **Fancy bonemeal, not a machine.** A hand-applied use-item, not a Vegetation Seeder. Soft divergence
   from P2.4-R's "one machine per rung", recorded and accepted: the action is still paid (compost
   economy) and bounded (grass only). The Seeder stays on record as the later automation tier.
2. **Range = vanilla bonemeal.** Same ~7x7 footprint, same weighted scatter, same attempt count. No
   budget, no custom radius. ("It would have a range in real life, not a budget.")
3. **3-second ripple.** Placements are revealed outward from the click, ordered by distance, over 60
   ticks - the "life spreading" camera beat. Vanilla bonemeal is instant; the reveal is the only
   interaction change.
4. **The plants are the state.** No `vegetated` flag anywhere. Plants do not decay and do not spread on
   their own. Break them (by hand or by encroachment) and the ground is no longer vegetated; re-fertilize
   to regrow.
5. **Grass-only gate.** No-op, and no item consumed, on coarse dirt or any non-grass. You cannot skip
   rung 1.
6. **Plants = frontier_cover.** Added to `#recompile:frontier_cover`. The encroachment sweep already
   strips a cover block before reverting grass (`RCEncroachment`, tested for vanilla poppy/short_grass),
   so placing our plants in the tag is the whole integration.
7. **Grass drop rules.** Hand-break destroys (no drop); shears retrieve the plant block. Block-items so a
   sheared plant re-places. No fiber drop (avoids a compost perpetual-motion loop).
8. **No crop bonemealing.** The custom use-item handles grass -> plants only; crops are out of scope (and
   would be extra code, not less, since we are not extending vanilla `BoneMealItem`).

## The plants

A weighted pioneer set - the hardy weeds that colonize rubble and brownfields, plus one mod-signature
hero. Cross-model sprites (X-shaped, cutout, no collision) like `dump_mushroom`; survival is normal
plantable-ground (the reclaimed-only constraint lives at *placement*, not survival, to avoid pop-off
churn). Textures generated by texgen.

| Plant | Role | Scatter weight (start) |
|---|---|---|
| `weedgrass` | bulk filler (the tall-grass analog) | ~60% |
| `dump_dandelion` | yellow flower pop | ~20% |
| `fireweed` | tall pink spike, vertical accent | ~15% |
| `can_sprout` | HERO - a weed growing out of a crushed can | ~5% |

Art status: 5 candidates each generated under `gen/texgen/<plant>-<date>/`, pending select + promote.

## Config (`RCConfig`, all gated; defaults are the design)

- `vegetationEnabled` (bool)
- `fertilizerRadius` (int; default matches bonemeal, ~4)
- `fertilizerAttempts` (int; scatter density, default ~64)
- `fertilizerRippleTicks` (int; default 60 = 3s)
- per-plant scatter weights (weedgrass / dandelion / fireweed / can_sprout)

## Non-goals

- No auto-spread, no auto-decay, no `vegetated` blockstate.
- No crop-growth acceleration.
- No Vegetation Seeder machine (that is the later automation tier).
- Fertilizer -> farmland is the separate Farming thread, not this spec.

## Phases

### Phase 1 - The dump plants (blocks + art)   [PR 1]

**Goal:** the 4 plants exist in the world and behave like grass. No Fertilizer wiring yet (place via
creative / `setblock` for testing).

- Select + promote the 4 textures (texgen).
- 4 plant blocks: cross-model, `noOcclusion`, no collision, instant-break, survive on plantable ground,
  map color PLANT/GRASS.
- Cross models + single-variant blockstates (random Y rotation for variety), `items/` client defs,
  block-items in `RCItems`, `models/item` (generated, layer0 = block sprite).
- Loot tables: shears-or-nothing (mirror `short_grass`).
- `#recompile:dump_plants` tag (the set) for later reuse; whatever vanilla plant tags are needed for
  placement/replaceable behavior.
- Lang; `RegistryCompletenessTests` coverage (name / client def / blockstate / loot / item form).
- GameTests: a plant survives on grass, breaks by hand with no drop, shears drops itself, and does not
  survive floating / with no ground.

**Exit:** 4 plants placeable and breakable in-world, art approved, suite green.

### Phase 2 - Fertilizer the scatterer (use-item + ripple + cover)   [PR 2]

**Goal:** Fertilizer greens reclaimed grass over 3s, and the result defends the frontier.

- Convert `RCItems.FERTILIZER` to a `FertilizerItem` with `useOn`: on grass, compute the bonemeal
  footprint, roll the weighted plant mix, and schedule placements over `fertilizerRippleTicks` ordered by
  distance from the click (the ripple). Consume one; particles + sound. No-op and no consume off-grass.
- Ripple mechanism: a lightweight scheduled process (scheduled block ticks or a small transient
  scheduler) - no BlockEntity, no saved state. If the chunk unloads mid-ripple the remainder is dropped
  (acceptable; the timing is cosmetic). Cells already holding a plant are skipped (no overwrite).
- Add the 4 plants to `#recompile:frontier_cover`.
- Wire the config.
- GameTests: fertilizer on grass places plants within the footprint; on coarse dirt does nothing and is
  not consumed; the item is consumed on success; a placed plant is `frontier_cover` and the sweep strips
  it while sparing the grass (extend `EncroachmentTests`).

**Exit:** in-world, fertilizing reclaimed grass ripples plants in over 3s; encroachment eats a plant
before the grass. Suite green.

### Phase 3 - Polish + integration   [PR 3]

- JEI: a Fertilizer usage entry (Fertilizer + grass -> vegetation), like the other non-recipe mechanics.
- Balance defaults pass (weights, density, ripple) from a `runClient` session.
- Update `docs/roadmap.md` + `docs/mod_jam_roadmap.md`: mark Vegetation built.
- Full `build` + `runGameTestServer` + a `runClient` grey -> green -> flowering beauty-check (the M1 jam
  beat and a GIF-worthy moment).

**Exit:** the tier plays and reads on camera; suite green; roadmap updated.
